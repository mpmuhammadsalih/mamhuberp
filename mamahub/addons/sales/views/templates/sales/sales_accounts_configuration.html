Sales Accounts Configuration

Options that control how sales invoices and sales orders behave. Applies to all branches.

Minus billing

When enabled, the screen allows billing an item even when the store does not have enough stock, letting on-hand quantity go negative. When disabled, lines are blocked until sufficient stock is available. Sales Invoice and Sales Order are controlled independently.

Sales Invoice uses the MINUSBILLING flag; Sales Order uses MINUSBILLING_ORDER. Both apply to all branches. Users must refresh open Sales Invoice / Sales Order pages for the change to take effect.

Order No mandatory

When enabled, users must enter an Order No (Sales Invoice / Sales Order) or Order / Invoice Ref (Sales Return) before adding lines or saving. When disabled, the field is optional.

Stored in LOCATIONMASTER.ORDERNO_MAND_INVOICE, ORDERNO_MAND_RETURN, and ORDERNO_MAND_ORDER for all branches. Open Sales Invoice / Sales Return / Sales Order pages pick up changes automatically.

Direct posting (cash)

When enabled, cash / walk-in documents post the cash side straight to the Cash or Bank (card) GL account mapped on each payment method (POS Tender Types), instead of Customer Receivable. Credit documents always post to Receivable.

Stored in LOCATIONMASTER.SALES_DIRECT_POST (invoice) and SALES_DIRECT_POST_RETURN (return) for all branches. Tender GL accounts come from POS Tender Types (e.g. CASH → cash account, CARD / VISA → bank account). Invoice: Dr Cash/Bank, Cr Sales. Return: Dr Sales, Cr Cash/Bank.
