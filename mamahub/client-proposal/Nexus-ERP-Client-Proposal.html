3. Core Platform Modules

Included in the Core ERP Platform license. Scope can be trimmed for Phase 1.

Setup and Administration

Users, roles, tenants, branch/location, GL dimensions, fiscal year, database wizard, activity log.

Master Data

Customer, supplier, item master, brands/UOM/barcodes, salesman, route, employee, session.

Sales and Point of Sale

Cash/credit invoicing, returns, sales orders, POS billing, multi-tender settlement, QZ Tray printing.

Inventory and Warehouse

Stock by location, store transfers, bins, stock moves, visual stock, verification, cycle counting.

Purchasing and Finance

LPO, GRN, batch/lot, returns, AP posting, COA, journals, AR/AP, VAT, bank recon, P&L, balance sheet.

Head Office and Reports

Central masters, branch requests, cross-tenant stock, sales/purchase/stock/collection reports, dashboard KPIs.

Collaboration

Internal chat, outstanding notifications, PDT handheld setup, Tasveek online sync (optional).

4. AI Intelligence Suite Optional Add-on

AI modules are priced and implemented separately from core ERP. Adopt incrementally after operations stabilize.

AI Forecast Cockpit

Sales/purchase forecasting, reorder suggestions, product/supplier/customer analytics.

Smart List Assistant

Ask business questions in plain language. Live Oracle SELECT with role-aware guardrails.

Document Intelligence

GRN invoice extract (PDF/image), natural-language PO creation, temp invoice analysis.

Operational AI

POS verify insights, Arabic item name assist, HO customer onboarding automation.
